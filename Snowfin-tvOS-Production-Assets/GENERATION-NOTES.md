# Artwork provenance

The two text-free scenic masters were created with the built-in image-generation tool, then deterministically cropped, resized, color-treated, and composited with exact vector/typographic branding.

## Top Shelf master prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Asset type: text-free master background for Apple tvOS Top Shelf, designed to be center-cropped to an extremely wide 3.22:1 banner. Realistic cinematic winter landscape at blue hour: a majestic snow-covered alpine mountain range reflected in a calm dark lake, restrained ethereal cyan/teal aurora across a deep navy sky, sparse fine snow particles, premium streaming-service key art. Extremely wide panoramic feel; horizon slightly below center; dramatic highest peak and brightest aurora on the right half; left 42% calm, dark, low-detail negative space for separately overlaid branding; coherent edge-to-edge scenery. Luminous icy moonlight; deep navy, black-blue, cool white snow, restrained ice-blue cyan. No people, buildings, animals, logos, text, watermark, frames, UI, or large geometric vector shapes.

## Icon background master prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: text-free master background for layered Apple tvOS home-screen and App Store icon. Cinematic deep-navy snowy alpine basin at blue hour, distant snow-covered mountain ridge and subtle frozen-lake reflection, restrained cyan aurora haze and tiny atmospheric ice particles, premium streaming app visual identity. Centered and symmetrical enough for a foreground logo; scenery soft and low contrast in the central 55%; no ridge line crossing the central logo area; full-bleed and safe to crop to 5:3. Deep, polished, dimensional, subtle depth; deep navy and black-blue with restrained icy cyan. No symbol, text, logo, watermark, border, or UI.
